// Case Study

KasiConnect: Bridging the
Informal Food Economy

1. The Problem: South Africa’s informal food vendors often rely on word-of-mouth, limiting their reach. High cash dependency increases security risks, while a lack of digital infrastructure prevents small-scale entrepreneurs from proving income for financial scaling.

2. My Role & Process: I independently developed this full-stack solution using an Agile methodology. The project progressed from a 7-day research phase to UI/UX design and a final 5-day intensive build, ensuring a balance between technical robustness and community accessibility.

3. Core Innovation: A unique dual-model approach allows sellers to choose between traditional direct-contact orders or a full online cart. This ensures the platform is inclusive for all levels of digital literacy.

4. Tech Implementation: Built with PHP, MySQL, and AJAX, the platform manages 17 interconnected tables for precise record-keeping. Real-time updates and responsive prototypes ensure a seamless experience across all devices.

5. The Result: Beyond e-commerce, KasiConnect facilitates digital transformation. By moving from paper-based systems to structured digital records, vendors can track performance, manage inventory, and unlock access to business funding.

Ultimately, KasiConnect empowers shisanyama masters and home cooks to grow sustainably, ensuring economic circulation remains within township communities.

// Capabilities

Key Features

Dual Vendor Models
Sellers choose between an "Online Marketplace" with full cart functionality or a "Call-Order" listing for legacy phone users.
AJAX Cart Integration
Custom JavaScript handles asynchronous 'Add to Cart' requests communicating with PHP, updating UI elements without page reloads.
Role-Based Dashboards
Dedicated interfaces for Customers, Sellers (managing listings/orders), and Admins (generating reports and moderating users).
Localized Discovery
Promotes nearby businesses by highlighting vendor names, exact pickup locations, and specialized township food categories.
Digital Record Keeping
Transitions informal sellers to structured databases, helping them build a track record of sales for future financial scaling.
Responsive Architecture
Fully optimized via CSS variables and grid layouts to ensure perfect rendering across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices.

// Backend

Database Architecture

The KasiConnect backend is powered by a robust MySQL database consisting of 17 interconnected tables that manage users, dual-seller products, and transaction records.

User Management (users, admins, customers, sellers)
Segregated tables to enforce role-based access. The system distinguishes between site administrators, regular customers, and two unique types of sellers. It also includes a password_reset table for secure tokenized account recovery.
Dual Product Listings (products, call_sellers_products)
To support our inclusive business model, KasiConnect maintains separate structures for standard e-commerce products (added to digital carts) and call_sellers_products (display-only items requiring a direct phone call to order).
E-Commerce Flow (orders, order_items, cart_items)
A fully normalized relational structure that tracks active cart sessions, finalized orders, and granular line items (who ordered what, and in what quantity), enabling accurate admin reporting and seller performance tracking.
